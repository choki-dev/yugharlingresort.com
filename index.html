Yugharling Resort & Wellness SpaWelcome to Yugharling

Bumthang has long been a place of spiritual reflection and inspiration. At Yugharling, we seek to reflect that same sense of calm through genuine hospitality, thoughtful service and spaces designed for rest and renewal.

Traditional Bhutanese craftsmanship, locally inspired interiors and energy-efficient technologies come together to create an experience that feels rooted in place while offering the comforts of a modern resort.

Sustainability

Bhutanese tradition, modern efficiencyComfort by Nature

Our approach to sustainability combines Bhutanese building traditions with modern efficiency. Locally produced mud bricks and traditional mud wash help the buildings sit naturally within the valley, while heat-pump hot water, Energy Recovery Ventilation (ERV), efficient climate control, LED lighting, waste reduction and locally sourced products support a more mindful way of operating.
